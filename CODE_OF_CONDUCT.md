# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent and discuss ideas, not people.
- Give and receive feedback professionally.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Disruptive trolling or bad-faith participation.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and interactions that violate this Code of Conduct.
